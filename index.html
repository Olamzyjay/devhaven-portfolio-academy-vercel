About DevHaven Studio

I am Olamide Joshua Olawuyi, and I use the internet, code, design, and marketing to solve real digital problems.

I am Olamide Joshua Olawuyi, the lead behind DevHaven Studio. I am a digital marketer, freelancer, developer, designer, and digital skills trainer who has spent years learning through deep internet research, practical experiments, and real client work.

What some people once saw as too much time online became the foundation of my skillfulness. I have explored ethical hacking, web systems, marketing strategy, funnels, ecommerce, SEO, and development, then turned that passion into a way to help people build, fix, sell, launch, and grow online.

My work covers web development, programming, UI design, site bug fixes, ClickFunnels-style sales funnels, Shopify and dropshipping stores, landing pages, form pages, webinar funnels, SEO optimisation, web and mobile app ideas, payment flows, and practical support for brands that need clean professional delivery.

DevHaven Studio is where I bring that commitment together: focused service, clear communication, and polished results for people who want their digital presence handled properly.

Delivery and training
